Ina fatan abokaina Furotesta, Katolika da Othodoks da suke kallon wannan za su yi tunani game da muhimmancin labari, da kuma yadda zai iya ba mu sarari don mu shiga cikin waɗannan ra'ayoyi masu kyau sosai game da yanayin Allah. Ina tambayar kaina, shin neman Allah nawa yana sa ni jin ƙarin yunwa ga kalmomi da rayuwar Yesu? Ina fatan haka ne.

![Jonathan yana magana game da Lewis da The Last Battle](/images/how-a-stable-holds-more-than-we-could-ever-imagine.jpg)

Sunana Jonathan. Yesu ne mutumin da ya canza rayuwata, kuma labarin Aslan a cikin The Chronicles of Narnia yana da muhimmanci sosai a gare ni, saboda wannan ita ce kwarewata ta farko game da yanayin Allah. Ban girma a cikin iyali mai addini ba, kuma ina gode wa Allah yadda labari ya ba ni sarari don in fahimci Allah, da kuma in ji ƙishirwa ga ƙari.

Aslan, musamman a cikin The Last Battle, yana da tasiri na musamman a yadda nake ganin Allah da duniya da har abada. Akwai wani wuri inda Lucy da Digory da Tirian suka fuskanci wani zaɓi mai wuya, yadda za su yi yaƙi har ƙarshe da jaruntaka a kan abokan gabansu.

Akwai barga a cikin labarin. A cikin wannan barga a zahiri akwai wani abu da ya fi yawa. Ga waɗanda suke da mummunan nufi, abin da yake cikin barga shi ne mafi munin mafarkinsu. Ga waɗanda suke da sha'awar sanin Allah, abin da yake faruwa a wannan wuri yana da zurfi sosai.

Lucy ta faɗi haka, bayan ta lura cewa abin da yake cikin barga ya fi abin da aka gani daga waje girma sosai. A duniyarmu ma, barga ta taɓa ɗaukar wani abu a cikinta wanda ya fi duniya baki ɗaya girma. Akwai wani abu game da Yesu wanda ya fi duniyarmu baki ɗaya girma, ya fi wanzuwarmu girma, ya fi sararin samaniyarmu girma, duk da haka an haife shi a cikin barga.

Ka yi tunanin kanka a cikin wannan yaƙi, inda Lucy ta gano farin cikin zuciyarta a cikin abin da wasu suka ga a matsayin wani yanayi mai muni, mai rufe ido. Ba su iya ganin har abada mai girma a cikin wannan barga ba. Ina fatan ba za ka rasa shi ba.
