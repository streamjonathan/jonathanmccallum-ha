Idan na duba baya ga yarintata, waɗannan littattafai su ne mabuɗin tafiyata ta bangaskiya, bin Yesu, da fahimtar wanene Yesu.

![Jonathan yana magana game da Narnia a cikin koren fili](/images/narnia-what-id-like-everyone-to-know.jpg)

Akwai wani abu mai ban mamaki a sanin Allah ta cikin labari, sannan a karanta a cikin Littattafai Masu Tsarki yadda yake da gaske, a cikin Yesu. Mutanen da ba su son yadda Lewis ya nuna Allah a matsayin Aslan ba sa son yin magana da ni game da Allah, a cikin Kiristanci ko a waje. Wannan yana faɗin abu mai yawa. Lewis ya yi tasiri a kaina sosai har na so in san wanene Yesu bayan na karanta The Chronicles of Narnia.

Aslan yana da kyau sosai har ba zai yiwu a nuna shi ta hanyar da ta bayyana gaba ɗaya abin da Lewis yake nufi ba. Lewis ya yi iya ƙoƙarinsa don ya bayyana wanene Allah.

Narnia ba labarin misali ba ne. Tunani ne game da wani yanayi na daban — duniya inda Yesu zaki ne, duniya da ba ta faɗi ba, inda za ka iya haɗuwa da Adamu da Hauwa'u na farko. Na ji daɗin shirin BBC. Kahon Faransa yana kama tunanina, zuciyata, duk lokacin da na ji shi. Idan ba ka karanta Lewis ba, ka gwada The Great Divorce, game da sama, da The Space Trilogy — littattafai biyu na farko suna da kyau sosai.
