Yesu yana tafiya a haikalin, a farfajiyar Sulemanu. Shugabanni suka taru a kusa da shi suka ce, "Har yaushe za ka bar mu cikin shakka? Idan kai ne Almasihu, faɗa mana a sarari."

Yesu ya amsa, "Na faɗa muku, amma ba ku gaskata ba. Ayyukan da nake yi da sunan Ubana suna shaida a kaina."

Sai ya faɗa a sarari: "Ni da Uba ɗaya ne."

Shugabanni suka ɗauki duwatsu kuma. Suka ce yana zagin Allah — yana mai da kansa Allah. Yesu bai janye ba.

Ya faɗi wanene shi, a sarari. Suka fahimci ainihin abin da yake nufi.

Me kake tunani game da wannan da'awa? Idan shi da Uba ɗaya ne, me zai canza? Kuma don me kake tunanin suka ɗauki duwatsu?

*Kalmomin Littafi Mai Tsarki a nan an fassara su don wannan shafin.*
