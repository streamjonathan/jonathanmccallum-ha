Ka taɓa ganin wanda yake buƙata ka wuce? Sannu ga duk wanda ke karanta Hausa — a duk inda kake, ina farin cikin kana nan.

Attajiri yana sanye da tufafi masu kyau yana cin abinci mai kyau kowace rana. A ƙofarsa, Lazaru matalauci yana kwance cike da ciwo, yana sha'awar cin ragowar abinci da ya faɗi daga teburin attajiri. Dukansu sun mutu. An ɗauki Lazaru zuwa ƙirjin Ibrahim, attajiri yana shan wahala. Attajiri ya roƙi ruwa kaɗan, amma Ibrahim ya ce, "Ka karɓi abubuwa masu kyau a rayuwarka, Lazaru ya karɓi marasa kyau. Yanzu ana ta'aziyya gare shi kai kuma kana shan wahala." Attajiri ya roƙi a aika wani ya gargaɗi 'yan'uwansa, amma Ibrahim ya ce, "Suna da Musa da annabawa; su ji su."

Wane ne a ƙofarka yanzu? Me ya kamata ka ji?

Idan kana karanta wannan da Hausa — a duk inda kake a duniya — za ka iya ɗaukar ɗan lokaci ka gaishe Jonathan?

*Kalmomin Littafi Mai Tsarki a nan an fassara su don wannan shafin.*
